About

I'm a fourth-semester master's student in Applied Mathematics at Universidad Industrial de Santander (UIS), with a background in Systems Engineering. My research is in hyperspectral thermal infrared remote sensing, focused on inverse retrieval methods for recovering atmospheric and object properties from hyperspectral data. I also teach — I've been instructor of record for Differential Calculus at UIS and have taught a thermal hyperspectral imaging course.

Research

Jul 2025 — Present

Research Assistant — AFOSR-SOARD Grant

Universidad Industrial de Santander (UIS). Funded by the Air Force Office of Scientific Research, South American Office of Aerospace Research and Development (AFOSR-SOARD).

  • Developed a HITRAN-based line-by-line atmospheric forward model for robust passive LWIR ranging, validated against RADIS and SpectralCalc.
  • Built joint inverse-retrieval solvers (Joint, Alternating Block-Coordinate Descent, and Null-Space Schur Complement) to disentangle atmospheric nuisance parameters from range and object properties — the basis of my M.Sc. thesis.
  • Working on simultaneous inverse retrieval of atmospheric parameters (air temperature, water vapor concentration) and object properties (emissivity, temperature) from single hyperspectral pixels.
  • In collaboration with Dr. Wellesley Pereira, also working on camouflaged target detection in spaceborne hyperspectral imagery, using GHOSt-04/05 satellite datasets (locations include Salon-de-Provence, Rena, Porton Down, and Fort Walker).
